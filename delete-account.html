Delete Your Account

Driver Directory

You can permanently delete your Driver Directory account and everything attached to it. There are two ways to do it.

In the app — immediate

  1. Open Driver Directory.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm.

Deletion happens straight away. No waiting, no email needed.

By email — if you cannot sign in

Send a message to tahaahmadmirza4411@gmail.com from the email address you used in the app, with the subject "Delete my account".

We must receive it from that address so we can be certain it is you. We will confirm within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Permanently, and it cannot be undone:

  • Your account and email address
  • Your profile — name, phone number, home area
  • Your saved favourites
  • Your call history
  • Any driver application you submitted
  • Every document you uploaded — CNIC, driving licence, vehicle registration book

What may remain, and for how long

If you were listed as an approved driver, your public listing is removed at the same time and no longer appears to anyone.

Encrypted backups may retain a copy for up to 30 days before they are rotated out. Those backups are not accessible to the app or to other users, and are used only to recover from a system failure.

We keep no other copy of your data after deletion.
