Privacy Policy

Driver Directory · Last updated 24 November 2026

Driver Directory helps parents and teachers in Wah Cantt find verified pick & drop drivers for school students. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to get it deleted.

This app is operated by Taha Ahmad. For any privacy question, contact tahaahmadmirza4411@gmail.com.

What we collect

You can browse the driver directory, search it and call a driver without an account. We collect nothing until you sign in.

What is public and what is not

Identity documents are never public. CNIC numbers and document photos are stored in a private bucket and are readable only by the directory administrator reviewing your application, through short-lived links that expire after five minutes.

If your driver application is approved, the following becomes visible to anyone using the app: your name, vehicle make, model, year, colour and registration, the areas and schools you serve, your availability, and your phone number — because parents need to call you. Do not apply if you do not want your phone number listed.

Your favourites, call history, email address and identity documents are never shown to other users.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not share it with advertisers.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party analytics in the app.
  • We do not track your location. The app has no location permission.

Who processes your data

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database, authentication and file storage, and acts as a processor on our behalf. Sign-in is handled by Google. Data is protected by row-level security rules so that one account cannot read another's records.

How long we keep it

  • Rejected driver applications and their documents are deleted within 90 days of the decision.
  • Approved driver listings are kept while the listing is active.
  • Favourites and call history are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Everything is removed immediately when you delete your account.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Profile → Delete My Account. This permanently erases your profile, favourites, call history, any driver application you submitted, and every document you uploaded. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, request deletion here: Request account deletion.

Children

This app is for adults — parents, teachers and drivers. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. The app stores no information about the students being transported.

Your rights

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. Write to tahaahmadmirza4411@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how we use data you already gave us, notify you in the app.
